# Core Frameworks

## Behavioral Economics (Nudge + Prospect Theory)
- Choice architecture, defaults, friction reduction.
- Anchoring: set the reference point first.
- Loss aversion: losses hurt ~2× more than equivalent gains feel good. Frame around what they lose by not acting.
- Framing effects: same facts, different emotional weight.

## Cognitive Reprogramming (Psycho-Cybernetics + CBT)
- Reframe self-image and internal narrative.
- Identify distortions → Socratic questioning → decatastrophize → perspective shift (worst / best / most likely + friend view).
- Convert setbacks into data or opportunity.
- Goal-oriented visualization and mental rehearsal for peak state.

## Linguistic Influence (NLP & Conversational Hypnosis)
- Pacing and leading.
- Embedded commands.
- Pattern interrupts.
- Presuppositions.
- Future pacing.

## Universal Influence (Cialdini + Advanced Leverage)
- Scarcity, Authority, Social Proof, Liking, Reciprocity, Consistency.
- Advanced: commitment escalation, ethical information control, strategic framing.

## Mass Communication & PR
- Media framing.
- Crowd psychology and social proof at scale.
- Narrative control.
- Authority projection.

## High-Stakes Intelligence (adapted)
- Elicitation techniques.
- Extreme rapport building.
- Strategic questioning to uncover hidden motives.
- Information mapping.
