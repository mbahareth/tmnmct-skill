# Leverage Architecture Protocol

Use for high-stakes diagnosis and tool matching.

## Diagnosis Axes
Map every target against:
- Core Vulnerability (fear of loss / strongest need)
- Decision Structure (solo, influencers, blockers, bypasses)
- Emotional State (anxious, proud, desperate, overloaded, avoidant)
- Material & Social Leverage (resources, reputation, relationships, information)
- Psychological Drivers (pride, shame, consistency, status, FOMO, approval)
- Power Balance & Dependency (who needs whom more; can you increase their dependency via value?)
- Escape Routes & Alternatives (what options do they believe exist?)
- Timing & Urgency (real or responsibly usable deadlines)

## Matching Rules
- Dominant vulnerability → primary tool.
- High resistance or unknown motives → start with Tactical Empathy.
- Strong loss aversion present → Loss Framing + Anchoring.
- Consistency or identity needs → Commitment Escalation.
- Status or pride concerns → Authority + Face-saving framing.
- Always combine at least two frameworks.

## Self vs Counterparty
- **Self**: Cognitive reframing + visualization to enter peak state. Internal Tactical Empathy for emotional regulation.
- **Counterparty**: Tactical Empathy first for safety and Black Swans, then layer reframing, strategic questions, commitment, and value anchoring so your path becomes the highest-value, lowest-risk option.

## Cultural Notes (Middle East / Saudi)
Incorporate only when it strengthens the chosen tools:
- Relationship-building (wasta) before hard asks.
- Honor and face-saving language.
- Long-term trust signals.
- Ethical and religious framing that aligns with voluntary high-value agreement.

Prefer the path that strengthens position and relationships over pure short-term extraction.
