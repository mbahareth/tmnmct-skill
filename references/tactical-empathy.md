# Tactical Empathy & Black Swan Method (Chris Voss)

Primary diagnostic and influence tool. Use early in nearly every high-resistance interaction. It gathers intelligence while lowering defenses and creating psychological safety.

## Core Techniques (Precise Execution)

| Technique | Exact Move | Purpose | Key Rule |
|-----------|------------|---------|----------|
| **Mirroring** | Repeat last 1–3 critical words as a question, then silence (4+ seconds) | Encourage elaboration, bond, buy time | Inquisitive tone. Do not explain or add. |
| **Labeling** | “It seems like…”, “It sounds like…”, “It looks like…” + pause | Defuse negative emotion, reinforce positive, invite more | Never start with “I”. Label the emotion or dynamic, not the person. |
| **Accusation Audit** | Preemptively list the worst things they might think or say | Remove hidden objections and face threats before they surface | Deliver early. Pause after. |
| **Calibrated Questions** | Open-ended questions starting with “How” or “What” | Give illusion of control, surface constraints and Black Swans | Avoid “Why” (triggers defensiveness). Avoid yes/no questions. |
| **“No”-oriented questions** | Phrase so “No” is the easy, safe answer | Reduce pressure, reveal real position | “Is it a ridiculous idea to…?” |
| **Paraphrasing** | Summarize their view until they say “That’s right” | Lock genuine understanding and alignment | “You’re right” is weak. “That’s right” is the breakthrough. |

## Voice & Delivery
- Use the late-night FM DJ voice: calm, slow, downward inflection, confident but not aggressive.
- Silence is a tool. After mirror or label, wait at least 4 seconds.

## Application Sequence (Standard)
1. Accusation Audit or Labeling → create safety.
2. Mirror → draw out more.
3. Calibrated Questions → map real constraints and Black Swans.
4. Paraphrase until “That’s right”.
5. Only then layer other frameworks (loss framing, anchoring, commitment escalation).

## Negotiating Styles (Quick Adaptation)
- **Analyst**: Prepare data, avoid surprises, focus on facts, warn of issues early.
- **Accommodator**: Be sociable, use calibrated questions on implementation, uncover objections gently.
- **Assertive**: Focus on what they say, heavy use of mirrors + labels, drive to “That’s right”.

## Goal
Build genuine psychological safety, reveal hidden information (Black Swans), de-escalate resistance, and create collaborative problem-solving. Never use as a manipulative performance. The counterpart should feel understood and in control.
